Access and Login
=================

Accessing the Application
--------------------------

To access the web application, use the official link provided via email or internal communication.  
Open the link in your preferred web browser (e.g., Chrome, Edge, Firefox).

Login Screen
------------

Once the login page loads, you will see the standard login screen.

.. image:: /_static/login_page.png
   :alt: Login page
   :width: 250px
   :align: center
   :class: soft-edge


- **Username**: Enter your assigned username or email ID.
- **Password**: Enter your secure password.
- **Login Button**: Click to log into the application.

.. important::

   You must check the **Terms and Conditions** checkbox to proceed with login.

Logging In
----------

After entering your credentials and click on **Login** and you will be redirected to the **Landing Page (Dashboard)**.

From there, you can begin navigating through the application's features.

.. note::

   - Do **not** share your login credentials.
   - Always log out after completing your session.
   - Use a modern browser for best performance and security.


Troubleshooting
---------------

If you experience issues during login (e.g., incorrect credentials, page not loading):

- Contact your **system administrator** or **support team**.
- Provide your username (if available) and a brief description of the issue.
- Include a screenshot if possible.
